INSIDE THE BOOK

More than a list of Prime Ministers.

Each era is studied across multiple dimensions so readers can understand the period in context.

01

Government & Politics

Government formation, elections, coalitions, leadership and major political developments.

02

Wars & Security

Wars, conflicts, border crises, terrorism, defence policy and major capability developments.

03

Economy

Growth, inflation, employment, trade, fiscal policy, reforms and available development indicators.

04

Laws & Welfare

Major laws, constitutional changes, welfare programmes and social-sector initiatives.

05

Infrastructure

Railways, roads, power, dams, ports, airports, industry, nuclear and space projects.

06

Foreign Relations

Major diplomatic relationships, treaties, crises, international organisations and strategic developments.

07

Science & Technology

ISRO, nuclear programmes, technology, research institutions and national scientific milestones.

08

Controversies & Cases

Allegations, investigations, charges, court findings, audits and commissions are kept distinct.
